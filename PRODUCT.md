# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners of Spanish small businesses (2-30 people) that already sell and have daily operations: customers who
repeat, follow-ups, invoices, a WhatsApp that never stops, social media to keep alive. Any sector: clinic,
academy, workshop, shop, online store, lodging, professional office. They have heard «digitaliza» for years,
distrust agencies that sign, charge and vanish, and mostly arrive by recommendation; the website confirms or
kills the trust before they write. They read it on the phone, often at night.

## Product Purpose

Padrón IA puts a business in order so it sells more and loses less, and stays to keep it working. The site
must make an owner think «this is exactly my mess, and they can fix it» and write on WhatsApp to ask for the
free business review. Success = WhatsApp conversations started from the site.

## Positioning

A trusted person who puts your business in order and stays (the category the owner already knows: «like my
accountant, but for the day to day»), not an agency and not «AI for SMEs». The proof is that the systems can be
seen working; no competitor in Spain shows that.

## Operating Context

What we put in order, process by process: customers who are slipping away (alert before they are lost) ·
winning customers back · follow-up of every customer · invoicing done and the quarter ready for the gestoría ·
social media prepared and scheduled · WhatsApp answered at once, any hour · a panel of how every customer is.
Offer ladder: free business review (30 min + three fixes in writing within 48 h) → «Puesta en orden» (closed
price and deadline, paid by milestones) → «A tu lado» (monthly fee, no lock-in). Contact: WhatsApp
+34 624 66 49 07 (answered by Lía, the assistant) and hola@padron-ia.es.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static Astro site served by nginx; a live «pulse» of real systems already exists (`src/components/PanelVivo.astro`).
- Prices are not decided: never show figures; the shape («closed price + monthly fee, no lock-in») is allowed.
- Spanish (Spain) only. Analytics is cookieless Umami (no consent banner). Legal pages exist and stay.
- Search: keep «consultor de IA» in titles; URLs `/`, `/tienda`, `/escuela`, `/despachos`, `/legal`,
  `/privacidad`, `/cookies` must not break.

## Brand Commitments

- Name Padrón IA. Indigo identity (Design System artifact, 17-sep-2026): ink `#1B1E3A`, lavender `#8F9BFF`,
  bone `#F2F3FB`, pearl `#C9A86A`. Typefaces are open to change.
- The brand speaks as «nosotros»; Jesús Martínez Padrón appears by name and face as the person who looks after
  your business.
- Never the word «automatizar» or «IA» as the promise; sell what the owner gains (sell more, lose less, peace
  of mind), not the method. No fear headlines. No agency look (logo walls, 25 services).
- Your data is always yours; the code and the app belong to Padrón IA (licence).

## Evidence on Hand

- Live pulse of real systems (PanelVivo). Working systems behind every process listed above.
- Talk about capacity, not today's count («works the same with 10 customers as with 1,000»).
- No client names on the public site (OFM never appears). No testimonials yet: none may be invented.
- Jesús's photo: pending; keep a marked slot.

## Product Principles

1. Show it working, don't describe it.
2. Every owner must see their own mess in it, whatever the sector.
3. Lower the fear: a person with a name, no lock-in, your data is yours, a small first step.
4. One action: write on WhatsApp for the free review.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA; full content readable with motion reduced or animation failing; phone first.
